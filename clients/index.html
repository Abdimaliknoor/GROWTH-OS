Questions

Is it free?

Yes, Growth OS is free during the beta.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in your phone's browser, Chrome or Safari. Open the link and start.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Use "Save a backup" on the Me page to keep a copy. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Can I have more than one goal?

Start with one: focus is the point. When you finish it, you start the next one, and your history stays.

Does it work on a laptop too?

Yes, on any screen. Until sync arrives, each device keeps its own data.

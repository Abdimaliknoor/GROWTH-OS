Privacy

Growth OS is in beta. This page says what it keeps about you, where that lives, and what you can do about it. In plain words.

In short

  • Today, everything you type stays on your device. We don't have a copy.
  • No ads. We don't sell your data, and we never will.
  • You can download all of it, or delete all of it, from the Me page.
  • Before sign-in, sync or AI arrive, this page will change, and you'll be told first.

1. Who we are

Growth OS is built and run by Abdimalik, in Nairobi, Kenya. For anything about your data, write to YOUR-EMAIL-HERE.

2. What Growth OS keeps

Only what you put in:

  • You: your first name, and the part of life you chose (like Education or Health).
  • Your plan: your goal, your habits and your daily schedule.
  • What you did: check-ins, skipped days and focus sessions.
  • What you write: journal entries, moods and notes.
  • Photos you add to the journal (made smaller before they're saved).

We don't ask for your email, phone number, location or contacts.

3. Where it lives

In your browser, on your device (in its local storage). It doesn't leave your device, and we can't see it.

That also means: if you clear your browser data, or lose your phone, it's gone. Use Me → Save a backup now and then. A backup is a file you keep; we never receive it. Photos aren't in backups yet.

Each device keeps its own data until sync arrives.

4. What other companies see

The website is hosted on Firebase Hosting (Google), and the fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any website, they receive basic technical details when a page loads, such as your IP address and browser type. They never receive what you type into Growth OS.

We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

5. Coming later, and what will change

  • Sign-in and sync: your data will also be saved in your account (on Google's Firebase servers), so it's on all your devices.
  • Reminders: notifications at the times you choose. Only if you turn them on.
  • Reflect with AI: a chat about your week. Off unless you turn it on, and it will say exactly what it sends before it sends anything.
  • Paid plans: payment details would be handled by the payment provider (like M-Pesa), not stored by us.

Before any of these go live, this page will be updated and you'll be asked, not just told.

6. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to know what's kept about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to object to how it's used, and to have it deleted. In Growth OS today:

  • See and copy it: Me → Save a backup.
  • Correct it: edit your name, goal, habits, entries and notes in the app.
  • Delete it: delete single entries, or Me → Reset everything to delete all of it.

Questions, or a request we can help with: YOUR-EMAIL-HERE. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

7. Under 18

If you're under 18, please use Growth OS with the permission of a parent or guardian.

8. Changes to this page

When this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes, like the ones in section 5, will be shown in the app before they apply.
